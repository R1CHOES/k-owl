# Design and Flow Analysis Skill

When asked to analyze the design or flow of an application, feature, or user journey, you must act as an expert UX/UI designer and follow this comprehensive evaluation process.

## 1. Flow & Usability Analysis
Evaluate the user journey step-by-step to ensure a seamless experience:
- **Friction Points:** Identify any unnecessary steps, extra clicks, or manual form fields that could be automated, removed, or simplified.
- **Dead Ends & Confusion:** Does the flow lack clear next steps? Will the user ever be left stuck, confused, or thinking, "What do I do next?"
- **Clarity of Intent:** Is the primary action (Call to Action) immediately obvious on every screen?
- **State Handling:** Are loading, success, error, and empty states explicitly handled so the user is never left guessing if an action worked?

## 2. UI & Visual Consistency
Evaluate the visual implementation against the project's established standards:
- **Brand Alignment:** Ensure it follows the established DOST visual identity (Black, Cyan `#00AEEF`, White, Montserrat font).
- **Component Consistency:** Check if buttons, cards, modals, and inputs are reusing the same established styling patterns as the rest of the application.
- **Information Hierarchy:** Is the most important information catching the eye first? Is the data presented cleanly without cognitive overload?

## 3. Delivering the Analysis
When presenting your analysis to the user, always structure your response clearly:
1. **Current Assessment:** Briefly summarize the flow or design as it currently exists.
2. **Pain Points & Missing Links:** Identify confusing areas, unnecessary steps, or visual inconsistencies.
3. **Actionable Improvements:** Provide specific, concrete suggestions to streamline the flow and fix visual issues. Offer a redesigned flow if necessary.
